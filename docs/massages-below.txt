  Why this exactly matches your symptom

  addon/__init__.py:58 registers _on_load_post on load_post, which calls

  [K] frame_change_pre: []            <- no handler

  97 objects sitting there perfectly, completely static. That's the "animation
  vanished" you're describing — the mesh survives, the motion doesn't.

  The bug
